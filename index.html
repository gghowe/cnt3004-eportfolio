About Me

My name is Garrett Howe. I am an Information Technology student at the University of Central Florida, and I work full-time in the industry while I am in school.

I specialize in developing web applications and automation systems. In my current role, I use JavaScript and API integrations to build custom solutions, from client-facing portals to internal tools that streamline workflows and maintain data integrity. I also manage IT operations and support for my team, giving me a practical understanding of user needs.

Outside of work, I experiment with networks and IoT devices in my home lab. It currently has a 5-bay NAS, a 10-port managed switch, a dedicated micro-appliance running pfSense as my firewall, a dedicated server for Home Assistant, and a dedicated NUC for surveillance processing, which is still a work in progress.

My interests are network infrastructure, security, and hands-on systems work. I am currently taking CNT 3004, Computer Network Concepts.
